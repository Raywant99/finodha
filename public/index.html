System Architecture Overview

Finodha implements an institutional-grade financial ledger engine modeled after Zerodha's trading infrastructure and SEBI's Margin Trading Facility (MTF) regulatory framework.

POST /api/auth/login Rate-limited authentication endpoint returning JWT token.
GET /api/portfolio Calculates real-time portfolio valuation, SEBI haircuts, and borrowing headroom.
POST /api/portfolio/pledge Pledges or unpledges securities with LTV threshold verification.
POST /api/loans/apply Automated instant margin loan origination and disbursement with ledger debit/credit.
POST /api/loans/:id/repay Partial or full repayment processing with interest-first allocation.
GET /api/ledger/verify Mathematical verification of SHA-256 chained transaction blocks.
POST /api/margin/liquidate Automated liquidation trigger to cure margin shortfall and restore safe LTV.
